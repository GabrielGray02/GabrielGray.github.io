description: <p>For this project, I wanted to create a budgeting tool that was both free and robust. I chose to use Excel as the platform because I wanted to improve my skills with the software while building something practical.</p><p>The tool includes four main sheets, <strong>Transactions</strong>, <strong>Monthly Budget</strong>, <strong>Lifetime Budget</strong>, and <strong>Planning</strong>. The Transactions sheet allows you to record all of your purchases and sources of income. It also features a convenient search function to help you locate specific entries quickly.</p> <p>The Monthly Budget and Lifetime Budget sheets summarize your transaction data in one place, providing a clear breakdown of spending and income by category. This makes it easy to see where your money is going and how it changes over time.</p> <p>The Planning section is designed to help you map out your financial goals. You can use it to plan how you want to distribute your money across various categories. It also calculates your expected savings based on interest rates and monthly contributions, making it a valuable tool for long-term financial planning.</p> <p>If you'd like to try it out, feel free to download the tool here, <a href="https://docs.google.com/spreadsheets/d/11W48DHjXRh8gnvwXm_6NOQ_7zcyVgFgA/edit?usp=sharing&ouid=108963103083494463417&rtpof=true&sd=true">Click Here</a>.</p>
