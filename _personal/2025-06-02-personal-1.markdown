description: <p>For this project I wanted to create a budgeting tool that was free and robust. I decided to use excel to create the budgeting tool as I wanted to improve my excel skills. I included three sheets, transactions, monthly budget, lifetime budget, and planning. The transaction sheet lets you note all of your purchases and your income. It also has a search feature to help you find specific purchases. The monthly budget and lifetime budget both summarize your transaction in one place and let you see the percentage break down. Finally, the planning section lets you map out how you want to distribute your money. It will also show you the expected savings you will have based on interest rates and monthly contributions. It is very useful for long term planning.
